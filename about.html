Hi, I'm Ian

I'm a former tech design leader who once built tools to scale creativity at speed. Today my focus is smaller, slower, and more personal: my own creative practice. Instead of AI and systems built for many, I now work by hand, one line at a time. It's quiet work that rewards patience and attention.

I've learned that speed can be powerful, but it often comes at the cost of depth. What I wanted was the space to notice details, to follow ideas carefully, and to let the process shape the outcome.

This studio is built on patience, precision, and the belief that slowing down creates better work. It's a place for exploring ideas with care and for valuing process as much as results.
